Overview

The Web AR Face Filter is a cutting-edge augmented reality application that runs entirely in your web browser. Built using Zappar's WebAR technology, it detects your face in real-time and applies stunning visual filters that transform your appearance with interactive effects.

This project demonstrates the power of modern web technologies to deliver immersive AR experiences without requiring users to install any native applications. Simply open the link on your phone or desktop with a camera, grant camera permissions, and watch the magic happen.

Features

4 Unique Filters

Four distinct face-altering filters, each with its own visual style and interactive behavior.

No App Required

Runs directly in the browser using WebAR technology — works on both iOS and Android devices.

Real-Time Tracking

Advanced face mesh tracking ensures filters follow your facial movements with minimal latency.

Photo Capture

Capture and save your transformed look with the built-in snapshot feature.

Tech Stack

Zappar WebAR Three.js JavaScript WebGL Face Mesh API HTML5 CSS3

How to Play

  1. 1

    Open the Game

    Click the "Play Game" button above or visit the game link on your mobile device or desktop with a camera.

  2. 2

    Grant Camera Access

    Allow the browser to access your camera when prompted. Your privacy is respected — no data is stored.

  3. 3

    Choose a Filter

    Select from four available face filters by tapping/clicking on the filter options displayed on screen.

  4. 4

    Have Fun!

    Move your face, make expressions, and watch the filters react in real-time. Capture photos to share with friends!
